- **THE CHALLENGE METRICS ARE NOT WHAT THIS REPO WAS MEASURING.** Read [docs/isles26_official_metrics.md](docs/isles26_official_metrics.md) before trusting any historical number. The organizers rewrote `utils/eval_utils.py` on 2026-07-24: the five ranked metrics are Dice, absolute volume difference (mL), absolute lesion count difference, lesion-wise F1 = panoptica **Recognition Quality under one-to-one IoU >= 0.25 matching**, and **PR-AUC over a soft probability map**. Our `eval/metrics.py` matches lesions at 1-voxel any-overlap many-to-many (far more lenient, model-dependent bias of -0.07 to -0.08 on lesion-F1), and PR-AUC had never been computed at all. The official code is cloned to `work/isles26_upstream/` and **imported verbatim** by `eval/official_metrics.py` — never reimplemented. `eval/probe_panoptica_semantics.py` measures (not assumes) the semantics we depend on: connectivity is **26**, the 0.25 threshold is on **IoU** not Dice, matching is one-to-one, `rq = TP/(TP+0.5FP+0.5FN)`, and PR-AUC is invariant under any strictly monotone transform (so calibration cannot move it). Two edge cases bite: `compute_pr_auc` returns 1.0 on an empty ground truth **only if the soft map is exactly constant**, else 0.0; and empty-on-both scores `empty_value=1.0` for Dice and F1. Sweep/scoring pipeline: `scripts/analysis_eval_01_setup_official_venv.sh` (builds `.venv-official` with panoptica 1.0.1) then `analysis_nnunet_47_official_metric_sweep.sh` / `_48_stage_d507_probs.sh` / `_49_reduce_official_sweep.sh`. Keep BOTH harnesses: the official one **decides**, `eval/metrics.py` **explains** (size-binned recall, HD95/surface Dice, per-center strata). Divergences are pinned by `eval/tests/test_official_agreement.py`.
